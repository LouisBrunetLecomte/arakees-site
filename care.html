Arakees Care

Always on. Always protected.

Every platform comes with Arakees Care. We run it, secure it and look after it, so you can focus on your clients.

Availability

Hosted in the EU on redundant servers in two data centres, with automatic failover and round-the-clock monitoring. If one server stops, the other takes over.

Security

Security updates as soon as they are released, encrypted backups every night with two separate providers, regular restore tests and a yearly security audit.

Confidentiality

Each platform runs in its own isolated space. Your data is never sold or used for anything else, and is covered by a GDPR data processing agreement.

Personal follow-up

A real person who answers, small changes included, a quarterly check-in and a monthly report on your platform.

Always up to date

Fixes and improvements to your modules, and the legal updates your invoicing needs, without you lifting a finger.

Your data, your call

Export everything at any time. If you ever stop, you keep your licence and leave with all your data.

Behind the scenes

No single point of failure

Your website is served from a global network. Your data lives on a main server with a live copy in a second data centre, and encrypted backups sit with a second provider.

  • Global network for your website.
  • Live copy of your data in a second data centre.
  • Encrypted backups with two providers.
  • No open doors. Servers are reachable only through a secure tunnel.

Tested, not just promised

Failover and full restores are rehearsed regularly. A backup that has never been restored is not a backup.
