Privacy and cookies

What Arakees does with your data, in plain words. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

In short

  • This site presents Arakees. The only data collected is what you send us by email.
  • It is used only to reply to you. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The site sets no cookies.
  • You can get a copy of your data or have it deleted at any time by writing to contact (at) arakees (dot) com.

Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is the publisher of the site (see the legal notice). Contact: contact (at) arakees (dot) com.

Who has access to your data?

Only the publisher of the site, its host GitHub, Inc. (United States) and its email provider Zoho (European data centre) for the emails you send us.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies. Your browser only remembers, for the current tab, that you have already seen the opening animation; this is never sent to us.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and erase your data, to object, and to withdraw your consent. Write to contact (at) arakees (dot) com. You can also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).
