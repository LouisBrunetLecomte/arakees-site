FAQ

Questions, answered

When can I start?

Now. Write to us, we set up your platform for you and walk you through it.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup from €990, which includes your lifetime licence and can be paid in three instalments, then Arakees Care from €49 a month. Every plan includes every module, with no per-user fees.

Do I need technical skills?

No. We set up your platform for you, and you edit your own pages without a developer.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. Your website and client portal live at your own address, with your logo and colours.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Every plan already includes every module. You can move to a higher plan at any time and only pay the difference in setup.

Is my data safe?

Each platform is isolated from the others, hosted in the EU, backed up every night with two providers and monitored around the clock. You can export your data at any time.

What happens if I stop the subscription?

You keep your licence and receive a full export of your data. Your platform is paused, and you can bring it back without buying a new licence.

Does Arakees connect to my other tools?

Yes. Arakees works with Zapier and Make through API keys and signed webhooks, syncs both ways with Google Calendar and Outlook, takes card payments with Stripe and sends contacts to Brevo.

Which tools does Arakees replace?

Usually a booking tool, an invoicing tool, a document-sharing space and a separate website.
